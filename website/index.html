Is this a standard ERC-20?

Yes. IMDiddy Runner uses OpenZeppelin’s ERC-20 implementation. All 1,000,000,000 tokens are minted once to the constructor caller. During the IdentityMD launch, that caller is the factory, which distributes them. The token has 18 decimals.

Can someone mint more or freeze my tokens?

The deployed token has no owner, mint function, pause, blacklist, upgrade mechanism, or privileged burn. Holders can transfer their tokens and grant or revoke spending allowances.

Where can I buy it?

After the launch is confirmed, the race pass above shows the official contract, launch transaction, and buy link. The launch pairs IMDIDDY with IMD on Ethereum. Check the route, fees, and slippage in the trading app before approving a transaction.

Is this affiliated with Diddy or a baby-oil brand?

No. This is an independent parody meme project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sean Combs, Johnson’s, or any brand depicted in the supplied artwork.

What am I actually getting?

A meme token, not equity, a claim on revenue, or a promise of returns. Prices and liquidity can change, and you can lose the full amount you spend. The running club, oil, and race-pass language is part of the joke.
